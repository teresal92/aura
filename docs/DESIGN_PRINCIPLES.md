# Aura Design Principles

> **Draft.** These are early principles and will change.

**Working art direction:** Architectural restraint, literary soul, and a little romantic whimsy.

## The distinction

We are not trying to make a digital interface look like an antique book.

We are borrowing the sensibility of beautiful books: their composition, pacing, typography,
tactility, and intimacy.

## Principles

### 1. Structure before decoration

Strong grids, generous margins, deliberate proportions, clear hierarchy. The layout should feel
almost architectural.

### 2. Typography as the main character

Beautiful serif typography, thoughtful pairings, expressive scale, careful spacing. Type should
carry much of the personality.

### 3. Warmth through materiality

Parchment, subtle grain, natural textures, muted colors. Not artificial distressing or an overly
vintage filter.

### 4. Whimsy as punctuation

A tiny illustration, an unexpected flourish, a delicate border, an ornamental initial, a little
hand-drawn detail. Occasional moments of romance.

### 5. Restraint

Every decorative element should feel intentional. Nothing should compete for attention.

### 6. The field guide's eye

Borrow from vintage field notebooks: the feeling of something jotted down by hand, on the fly, the
moment it was noticed. Think dated entries, quick marginal notes, small-caps captions, and fine
ruled lines. Capturing a thought should feel as immediate as writing it in pencil. As with books,
take the field guide's way of looking, not its yellowed pages.
